NOTICES

Effective July 1, 2026 Statute Update

Please review the attached summary from the Board and refer to the linked House Bill below. The updated statutes are linked below and available on the Board Statutes Page.

Statute Changes as of July 1, 2026

HB 3934

Oklahoma Statutes as of 7-1-2026

 

 

The Board office will be closed for business July 27-31, 2026 for building maintenance.  Please make sure you plan accordingly for anything you may need from us that month.  We will not be processing mail and will process emails once we return to the office.  ALL Dentist and Dental Hygienist applications for the August 21st Board Meeting will need to be in the office no later than July 24th, 2026 (REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Along with these applications, they MUST have the National Practitioners Data Bank and any state verification of licensures to be considered for the agenda). 

 

*ALL DENTAL ASSISTANTS MUST HAVE A PERMIT BEFORE WORKING CHAIRSIDE AS OF NOVEMBER 1, 2025.*

 

 

*** As of 3/25/26 we were notified that the SCAM callers are now using Board Staff names.  Please notify the board by email to obod.board@ok.gov when you receive these calls with any details you are able to give.  Also, board staff will not be pushy or request money.  ***
