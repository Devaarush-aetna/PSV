Search for a license

The Maryland Board of Examiners for Audiologists, Hearing Aid Dispensers, Speech-Language Pathologists & Music Therapists provides online license verification. To begin a search, enter either the Last Name or the License Number. To do a wildcard search on the Last Name you can enter the first few characters in the name and click the search button. Search results may take a little time; please be patient.
