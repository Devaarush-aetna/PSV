Effective January 1, 2014, the Board no longer prints paper licenses.
This is considered the primary source for verifying a license.

The Maryland Board of Social Work Examiners provides online license verification. To begin a search, enter either the Last Name or the License Number ( no A or G required). You will be provided with a list of names with similar last names or a list of license numbers. To get detail information click the link View Details. Click the Reset button to clear the boxes of any information. To do a wildcard search on the Last Name you can enter the first few characters in the name and click the search button. Search results may take a little time; please be patient.
