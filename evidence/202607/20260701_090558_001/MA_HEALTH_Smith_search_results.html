Look up a License

Welcome to the Massachusetts Health Professions License Verification Site. This public verification site provides information on health licenses and registrations issued by the Bureau of Health Professions Licensure (BHPL) of the Massachusetts Department of Public Health (MDPH).

Here you may:

  • Check the status of a license/registration of an individual or business
  • Check the Drug Schedules of a Controlled Substance Registration (MCSR)
  • Generate a Verification of Licensure
  • Export license data to an Excel or CSV file

To download the license data export files, please go to the Download License Data page. For additional help with checking a health profession license, go to https://www.mass.gov/info-details/check-a-health-profession-license.
