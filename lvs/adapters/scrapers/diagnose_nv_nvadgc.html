Online License Search

The Nevada State Board of Examiners for Alcohol, Drug and Gambling Counselors maintains an online License Verification system of licensed professional counselors and certified interns. You may search by any combination of the licensee or intern's, city, or license number.

If you cannot find a record for the licensee or intern that you are looking for, please contact the Board at ADGC-Support@adgc.nv.gov or (775) 689-0563
