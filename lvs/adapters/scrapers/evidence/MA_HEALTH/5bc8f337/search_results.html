Lookup a License

Welcome to the Massachusetts Health Professions License Verification Site. This public verification site provides information on health licenses and registrations issued by the Bureau of Health Professions Licensure (BHPL) of the Massachusetts Department of Public Health (MDPH). Here you may:

  • Check the status of a license/registration of an individual or business
  • Check the Drug Schedules of a Controlled Substance Registration (MCSR)
  • Generate a Verification of Licensure
  • Export license data to an Excel or CSV file

To check the status of a license, choose a license board and/or license type from the dropdown and click Search.

To download the license data export files, please go to the Download License Data page.

For additional help with checking a health profession license, go to https://www.mass.gov/info-details/check-a-health-profession-license.

For more details on a specific license and to generate a Verification of Licensure, click on the hyperlinked license number from the search results grid.

To sort search results, click on a column header. To filter search results, click the filter icon on the column header, then enter the filtering criteria. The three lines become visible when hovering over the column header.

To export search results, click the three vertical dots at the top right of the search result grid, then select the option to export as either an Excel or CSV file from the dropdown menu.

To view a list of status definitions please view the Bureau of Health Professions Licensure License Status Definitions.
