Psychologist License Verification

The Maryland Board of Examiners of Psychologists provides online license verification. To begin a search, enter either the last name or the license number. To do a wildcard search on the last name you can enter the first few characters in the name and click the search button. Search results may take a little time; please be patient. For psychology associate registrations, please use the provided "Psychology Associates" link in the nav bar.
