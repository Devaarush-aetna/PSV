Our Mission

Our mission is to assure the consumer of qualified licensed professionals. In pursuing our goal, we provide a process by which consumers may file complaints against our licensees or persons practicing dietetics/nutrition without a license.

 

FIGHT FRAUD, WASTE & ABUSE! Contact the Louisiana Legislative Auditor (LLA) Hotline if you suspect the misappropriation (theft), fraud, waste or abuse of public funds by anyone. Toll Free: 1-844-50 FRAUD (503-7283) Online: ReportFraud.La
